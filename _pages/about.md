I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Department of Economics, Duke University with broad research interest in macroeconomics, finance, and public finance. My work seeks to understand how fiscal and monetary policy actions affect the portfolio decisions of the banking sector and how these adjustments, in turn, shape macroeconomic outcomes such as credit supply, investment, and output. Relatedly, I also study the role of financial inclusion in supporting policy objectives such as firm growth and the efficient allocation of resources, particularly in emerging markets where equity markets are relatively thin.

I completed my M.Sc. in Economics at New York University Abu Dhabi, where I ranked first in the program and received the highest score on my master’s thesis. I completed my undergraduate studies in Economics and Mathematics at the Lahore University of Management Sciences (LUMS), graduating with Distinction.

In my free time, I enjoy Punjabi music, cricket, studying the history of religious and political thought, and discussing the role of markets with friends while hiking.
